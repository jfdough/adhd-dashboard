⚡ 10-Min Dopamine Reset

Your brain is overstimulated. Work through these one at a time. Takes 10 minutes. You'll feel different after.

Step 1 · Right nowPut your phone face down. Close all tabs except what you're working on. Physical environment first.
Step 2 · 60 secondsName what you're actually feeling right now. Bored? Anxious? Avoidant? Scattered? Say it out loud or type it below.
Step 3 · 2 minutesDrink a full glass of water. Stand up. Roll your shoulders back. These aren't clichés — they physically shift your nervous system state.
Step 4 · 2 minutesWrite down the single next action on your most important task. Not the whole project — just the next physical step. One sentence.
Step 5 · Start the timerOpen the Focus tab. Set 25 minutes. Start it before you feel ready. The feeling follows the action, not the other way around.
